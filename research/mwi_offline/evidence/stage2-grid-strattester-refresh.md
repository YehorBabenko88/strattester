## Simulator fidelity and adapter obligations

- simulate_trade validates side/entry_kind only (execution.py:40-43). It does not validate finite positive policy prices/notional/fees/slippage, OHLC geometry, sorted/unique bars, interval continuity, availability clocks, or risk orientation. Adapter must validate before calling; negative/NaN values and arbitrary ambiguous_policy must refuse. Set every cost/risk field explicitly and pin SL_FIRST rather than rely on permissive defaults.
- Market fills use first bar with t >= decision_time; limit earliest is decision_time+bar_ms (:47-61). t semantics must be explicit in the adapter; normalized open_ms maps to t, and decision cutoff must occur before executable future bar. A forecast made from the bar close must not fill that same bar's open. Market mode otherwise permits same-t fill. Include own causal boundary tests.
- It accounts adverse entry/exit slippage (:34-38,63,104), opening SL gaps at open, TP gaps capped at TP (:67-97), same-bar SL_FIRST ambiguity, EOD liquidation (:101-108), two-sided fees (:107). This is deterministic OHLC simulation, not trade/depth replay. Same-bar policy other than literal SL_FIRST selects TP; reject unknown policies at adapter boundary.
- Default zero slippage is not an observed market cost. Policy has no bid/ask spread input, event-to-fill latency, funding, borrow, maker/taker differentiation, queue position, liquidity/impact/depth or volume-constrained partial fills. Do not label net_pnl as fully cost-adjusted economic edge. Reject unsupported requirements or mark missing costs UNKNOWN and research qualification incomplete; any sensitivity overlays must remain separately labeled, not second ledger/fill authority.
- Scale simulation has declared fraction legs/targets (execution.py:143-190), not exchange-liquidity partial fills. New stage2 need only reuse simulate_trade; do not add another simulator or account engine.
- Grid scientific_simulation.py:13-19 models fees/slippage/spread/latency/funding/min-fill; :36-44 derives fill/slippage by deterministic hash and funding proportional to horizon, and :66-82 subtracts proxies. These are configured synthetic stress approximations, not actual market funding schedules or L2-calibrated fills. Grid barrier_simulator.py:34-35 uses fixed fees/slippage without funding/depth. BookTapePaperLearner observes prices with fixed cost_bps (book_tape_paper.py:13,32-45); outage/staleness guards are not executable fills.
- Rich25eb quote_paper.py:41-79 checks bid/ask latency/funding and exposes source_authenticity NOT_EVALUATED, caller-supplied causal quotes, depth/actual fills/completeness missing, training/trading unauthorized. It is a different lane and absent main; it cannot justify richer economics for a main37ff OHLC adapter.
- Existing risk controls are local research constraints. simulate_trade has SL/TP/notional but no portfolio exposure cap, collateral/margin/liquidation, concurrent positions, drawdown circuit breaker or model authority. Put bounded strategy selection and frozen research risk validation in the new own namespace; do not claim live risk qualification or bypass owner controls.
